================================================================================
 GB200 RACK BRINGUP — FLAT PATH (rack + compute-tray nodes only)
 Source: cwctl rlcc workflows -n gb200-rack-bringup-v4 -d
         cwctl flcc workflows -n <ct-workflow> -d
 Scope:  CDUs, power shelves, and NVLink switches intentionally EXCLUDED.
         Only rack-level actions and what each compute tray (BMN / node) does.

 NOTE:   This is the HAPPY PATH. If nothing fails — every FLCC phase avoids its
         failState and every RLCC await-checkpoint clears — this is the exact
         route the rack and its compute trays take to reach 'ready'. Failure
         branches, retries, and triage detours are not represented here.
================================================================================

LAYERS
  RLCC = rack lifecycle controller. Its workflow is gb200-rack-bringup-v4.
         Its steps are called ACTIONS.
  FLCC = fleet lifecycle controller. Runs per BareMetalNode (compute tray).
         Its steps are called PHASES (each pins a node STATE).

The rack walks the RLCC actions top-to-bottom. At a handoff action the rack
tells every compute tray to run a named FLCC workflow, then an await-checkpoint
action blocks until every CT reaches that FLCC workflow's end state.
